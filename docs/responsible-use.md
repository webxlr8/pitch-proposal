# Responsible use

- **Facts:** never invent metrics, client names, testimonials, prices, guarantees or dates. Sender claims come from `proof.yaml`.
- **Evidence:** list a problem only when a screenshot shows it. Crop screenshots; never edit them.
- **Reference sites:** use them to understand functions only. Do not copy their name, logo, imagery, people or text into a proposal for another brand.
- **Stock photos:** free licences only (Unsplash, Pexels). Never present them as the client's own staff, premises or products.
- **Crawling:** public pages only, one request at a time, no logins, no form submissions, no bypassing bot protection.
- **Sending:** the skill creates files. It does not email, upload or publish anything without your go-ahead.
