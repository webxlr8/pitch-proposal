Website Refresh Proposal · Layout check
02 · Layout check

What needs fixing

We reviewed the site on desktop and mobile. The content is strong, but the layout gets in the way. These are the main problems, as they look today.

1
Mobile · Homepage
Text runs off the screen on phonesThe banner, band and intro are wider than a phone screen, so sentences are cut off on the right.
2
Homepage · Hero
A 6.5 MB hero image and a small sloganThe night photo is a 6.5 MB file, so the first screen loads slowly. The slogan is small and low on the photo.
3
Homepage · Band
Booking a load means a phone call“Call Us to Book a Load” is the only way to book. There is no quote or booking form.
4
Homepage · Testimonial
One anonymous quote, with a typoThe heading reads “What our customer say”, and the only quote is from “A happy customer”.
5
Services
A plain list with no detailThree services in a bullet list. No photos, no detail, no way to request a quote on the page.
6
Contact
A broken link and no contact formThe “Rates” link leads to a missing page (404). Visitors can only phone, fax or email.
