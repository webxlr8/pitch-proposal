# Writing the proposal

The reader is a busy client, often reading on a phone. They should understand each page in seconds.

- **Plain English.** Short sentences (under 20 words), active voice, one idea per sentence.
- **Their words.** Use the client's own service names and terms from their profile or site.
- **Headlines:** sentence case, 3–8 words, one highlighted phrase in `<em>`. Vary the shape; do not repeat "X & Y" on every page.
- **Bullets:** start with the thing in bold, then what it does. 3–6 bullets per block.
- **Numbers:** only from the profile, the client's material, or your crawl (page counts, forms found). Write sample numbers inside concept screens only.
- **No hype:** avoid "unlock", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "world-class", "elevate", "game-changer", "robust", "leverage", "synergy", "revolutionise", em dashes as decoration, exclamation marks.
- **Be kind about the current site:** criticise layouts, not people. "The contact form shows only a Send button", not "your form is broken and unprofessional".
- **Concept screens:** realistic sample content (names, dates, sample product prices are fine there) and a note that they are illustrations.
- **Closing:** next steps in 4 short steps. If the user wants no prices, the footer may say "Commercials shared separately".
